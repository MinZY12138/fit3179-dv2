## Interpretation limits

Student-to-teacher ratios are not class size, vacancy rates or proof of teaching quality. National FTE does not locate individual schools. Teacher survey estimates are weighted and can include early childhood settings. Percentages for reasons for leaving refer only to teachers intending to leave and permit multiple responses. Intentions do not measure actual exits. Repeated-year survey estimates do not track the same individuals. Cartogram distances are schematic; its geometry is a display transformation, not a change to statistical values.
